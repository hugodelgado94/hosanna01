How to read the colours

  • Blue — tense identifier: the augment, the reduplication, or the tense formative (σ, θη, κ). This is what tells you when and which voice.
  • Red — the ending: person and number on a verb, case and number on a noun.
  • Black — the lexical stem, the part that carries the meaning.

Breathing

  • ῾ rough — adds an h sound

What happens when letters collide

Stem ending + σ (future, aorist)

labialβ π φ+ σ →ψ
palatalγ κ χ+ σ →ξ
dentalτ δ θ ζ+ σ →σ

This is why a future can look nothing like its present: πέμπω → πέμψω, ἄγω → ἄξω, πείθω → πείσω.

Contract vowels

εειηοουω
ααᾳαωωω
εειειηουουω
οουοιωουουω

Stem vowel down the side, ending vowel across the top.

Transliteration is academic, not pronunciation: η = ē, ω = ō, υ = y, rough breathing = h, iota subscript written as i.
